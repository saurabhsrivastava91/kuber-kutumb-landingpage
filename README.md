# Kuber Kutumb — Landing Page

**Manage Today • Grow Together** · *Ghar ki har zimmedari, ek jagah.*

Landing page for **Kuber Kutumb**, the all-in-one Indian household app — family expenses,
budgets, bills and udhaar, kids' school and tiffin, home staff, health, and home services.

## Live site

Once GitHub Pages is enabled:
**https://saurabhsrivastava91.github.io/kuber-kutumb-landingpage/**

## What's here

- `index.html` — the complete landing page. **Single self-contained file**: all CSS, JavaScript
  and images are embedded inside it. No build step, no internet needed — open it in any browser.
- `assets/` — the logo crops used in the page (lockup, mark, app icon), kept for future edits.

## Sections

- **Hero** — headline, brand line and a phone mockup of the app dashboard.
- **Stats strip** — 5 hubs, 30+ trackers, one-tap reports.
- **Features** — Paisa · Parivaar · Bachche · Home Staff · Sehat · Ghar & Services.
- **Deep dives** — Money (expenses, budget, bills, udhaar), Family & kids, Staff & health, Home & services.
- **Ghar Assistant** — the AI companion with example questions and answers.
- **Privacy-first** — biometric lock, Hindi + English, family roles.
- **How it works** — three steps.
- **FAQ**, **Get the app CTA**, and footer.
