The meaning behind our name

More than a name. A way of life.

In Indian tradition, Kuber is associated with wealth and abundance. Kutumb means family — the people who make a house a home. Together, they express our belief that true prosperity is not only about money; it is also about time, peace of mind, care and togetherness.

Kuber Kutumb brings this timeless sentiment into a modern idea: give every family a clearer, calmer way to look after the home and the people in it.

Good to know

A few common questions

A quick introduction to the Kuber Kutumb concept.

What is Kuber Kutumb?
Kuber Kutumb is an Indian household-management app project. Its stated scope includes family expenses, bills, household staff, kids, home services and health.
Is it only for tracking money?
No. The vision goes beyond finances to household coordination and family well-being, so families can bring related responsibilities into one place.
Why the name Kuber Kutumb?
Kuber evokes prosperity and wealth in Indian tradition; Kutumb means family. The brand combines thoughtful resource management with the value of family togetherness.
Is the app available to download?
The project is in development. This page introduces its brand and product vision; it does not claim a Play Store release or a live public service.
